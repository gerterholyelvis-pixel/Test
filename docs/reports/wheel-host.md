# Wheel host (WHEELHOST)
1. A tall thin goblin in a top hat, waistcoat, bow tie and monocle, with a cane, stands left of the wheel on the Start/queue screen and spins it. Presentation only: `wh.result`, `SLOT`, the odds (20/20/20 ... King 1%) and the info card are untouched.
2. Files: `menuhost.js` (new, `GKHOST`, tagged WHEELHOST), `tests/wheelhost.test.js`, 5 small tagged hooks in `menu.js` (begin, step, tick, landed, spin ramp `wh.lead`), a `<canvas id="host">` + CSS block in `index.html`, `'menuhost'` in `CLIENT_JS`.
3. It is a 2D canvas figure (about 100 path ops, no textures, no WebGL, no lights, no new assets), drawn at 30 fps (20 on Low) and only while the wheel screen is up; the 3D title scene is unchanged.
4. Idle: breathing, a glance at the camera every ~6 s, a hat tip every ~9 s, an occasional cane tap. Start: 0.55 s wind-up, 0.22 s arm swing on the rim, then the wheel ramps up (it starts after `wh.lead`; the stop time is unchanged).
5. Sound uses existing audio.js names: `tick` per peg (throttled), `tickLast` on landing, then `uiHover` / `pickup` / `victory` / `gong` by tier. Rarity tiers by odds: common shrug, uncommon clap, rare cheer, epic cheer + hat toss, King bow; each has a coloured spark burst (0/9/16/24/38).
6. Reduced motion: no wind-up, no reaction or sparks, sound only. Low preset: 0.3 s wind-up, dpr 1, 20 fps, fewer sparks. Width <= 760 px: host hidden, layout unchanged. On wider screens the wheel is a little smaller (room for the host).
7. Tested: `tests/wheelhost.test.js` (odds, SLOT order and result guard pinned, tiers, poses finite and reachable); screenshots `docs/screens/wheel-host-pull.jpg`, `wheel-host-king.jpg` (King reaction forced via `GKHOST.landed(4,1)`). Not tested: real audio by ear, touch layouts, Low FPS measurement.
8. Merger notes: `menu.js` hooks are marked `// WHEELHOST`; if `uiorg.js` or the pause-menu agent moves the roll layout, keep the `#host` block in index.html and `wh.lead` in the spin ramp.
