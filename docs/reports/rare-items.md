# Rare items: a rarity ladder and 26 finds with their own mechanics

- **Ladder** (`rareitems.js`, Codex tab Rarity): one published table per source, in % of rolls over the six existing tiers: chest 60/25/10/4/0.9/0.1 (common..transcendent), mystery, hall, kill, heist (vault guards), merchant, treasure (relic, mythic+); tree rooms keep their own tiers. An empty rung (no common gear) is skipped and the rest renormalised: a chest weapon lands uncommon 62.5 / rare 25 / mythic 10 / legendary 2.25 / transcendent 0.25. Before: 45/33/16/6/1.2. True random, no pity.
- **Mapping**: "epic" = mythic (tier 3), "one-of-a-kind" = transcendent (tier 5). No new tier. Transcendent: at most one drop per kind per match (`m.rare`), not recyclable, not bankable (keep.js MAX_TIER). Tier 2-4 finds recycle to 2 parts at most and bank at the normal cost; none can be reforged.
- **26 items** (13 gear, 7 charms, 6 one-use): Echo Dirk, Gravedigger's Spade, Hungering Blade (+1.5 per kill), Leechfang, Wardbreaker Maul, Stormtongue (chain lash), Kingsbane (+40% vs the kill leader); Stonebark Shell, Mossback Hide, Mirrorscale Mail, Bloodpact Plate, Phasecloak (first blow each round passes through), Aegis of the Last Stand (a blow takes at most 25%); Bloodstone, Ironwill Knot, Grudge Stone, Four-Leaf Clover (luck), Seeker's Lantern (vault-ring goblins on the minimap), Thunder Tooth, Phoenix Ember (once a round, 1 HP); Stoneskin, Bloodlust, Storm Flask, Hourglass Sand, Phoenix Tear, King's Feast. Each has a desc and a lore line; art is in `itemart.js` (RARE-ART), all 26 baked and checked in the browser.
- **Mechanics** run on the server (`raresrv.js`, 9 RARE-HOOK lines in server.js hitTarget/useTick/giveItem/c4Stagger/dash). Multipliers apply before the x1.75 damage cap. Stats sit inside their tier band, the effect is a small edge (tools/balsim.js: all beat the tier below 0.73-0.97, lose to the tier above, fair inside the tier).
- **Announcement**: banner with tier colour and glow, a chime per tier (audio.js rareFind1-4), a sparkle burst, in-fight tags (`rareui.js`); other goblins get a faint corner line for a trophy (no name, no place).
- **Tests**: `tests/rareitems.test.js` (seeded 300k-roll samples against `dropChance` for 17 kind/source pairs, the cap, luck, keep/recycle, balance, art, Codex rows, mechanics on fake players) and `tests/rareitems-server.test.js` (find, Phasecloak, Phoenix, Echo, Storm Flask live). `npm run test:affected`, balance3, keep, affix, keep-server, gameinfo pass alone; under load bots-combat, rounds5, combat4, pace, matchmaking, act5-trees flaked and pass alone.
- **Also fixed**: main's bandage `case 'heal'` had `extra = ...; break;` swallowed by a trailing comment (heal fell through into regen; utility-solo failed). armour3 and knowledge tests now skip `rare` items.
- **Not done**: luck cannot touch chests, mystery rooms and shops (rolled at match start); the lantern and the announcement are verified in the browser pane only, not with two real players; legacy transcendent gear is still recyclable for its old yield; `npm run map` not rerun.
- **Merger**: items.js RARE-BEGIN, hooks in server.js, halls.js/halls5.js, act5.js, rooms5-trees.js (rollItem weighs `rw`); new files rareitems.js, raresrv.js, rareui.js (CLIENT_JS and index.html updated).
