# Bots use the whole game (BOTUSE)
Code: tagged `BOTUSE` blocks in server.js (counter `bu()`, home chores, curiosity, sends, spare gear) and vbx5.js (`tourHint`), small hooks in BOTS5 / BOTS6 / HALLS lines. Pure goal numbers in botgoals.js (`K.FORGE 8`, `K.GRAND 6.5/8.5`, junk pull).
- **Audit:** bots already did homes, trees, heists, vault, halls, merchants. Missing or fake: the pump and Elixir Screen ran from `botHomes` with no walking or range check, so now `chore5` walks to the pump and screen and sends `pumpUse` / `baseMsg` (range and rate gates apply). Only the fixed list pump, alarm, lock, recycler, snare, bench was bought (never guard, pad, sentry), no distill / re-arm / ammo, only Pump Leech was sent.
- **Added:** per-bot shopping list (easy 3 cheap ones, normal 7 of its taste, hard 8 with an early sentry, then pump / lock / recycler level 2); distill; re-arm and reload; 5 room sends (bell, jam, gas, rats, spike) at the room where it saw goblins or heard a fight; utilities crafted at the bench (bomb, trap, smoke, tonic, lockpick); a hurt bot heads for a heal font or stew hall; one cheap spare kept for the Gambler, auction block and Keystone bowl.
- **Rooms:** a bot tries each tree type and each heist room once even when its hint is weak (easy 45 %, normal 80 %, hard 95 %). The Press rouses a vat with elixir or waits, the Oak nook is entered through its arch (bots were stuck on the arch), and a heist room gets a station tour (`tourHint`: 2 looks per round, the station it has means for first, never mid-attempt).
- **Same paths, no cheating:** every action is `A5.act`, `H5.act`, `baseMsg`, `pumpUse`, `craft`, `recycle`, `buy`, `claim`; caps and gates unchanged. Only `GK_DEBUG` knob: `GK_ELX_SCALE` (sim pumps x4 because 30 s rounds are a quarter of a real round).
- **Measure:** `node tests/sim-botuse.js 20 30000 10 4` (20 all-bot matches, mix of easy / normal / hard, per feature: uses, bots, bots per level). Last run 140 bots: pump 507 collects, 9 of 9 upgrades bought (sentry only by hard), distill 22, all 6 sends (leech, bell, gas, rats, jam, spike), all 10 tree types and all 4 heist rooms used, 14 of 17 heist stations, hall cache 154 / container 48 / font 6 / stew 3 / whetstone 21, merchant, chest, mystery, crown claims 74. Easy bots: pump, alarm, guard or pad only, no sends or distills.
- **Still 0 in that run:** `grand.recycle` and `forge.craft` (their doors rarely open and home covers recycling), ram head and planks (3 metal, 2 wood), Keystone carving (metal and bone). They flicker between 0 and a few per run. `screen.rearm` / `screen.ammo` need an intruder, bots never raid, so a lab test covers them. `GK_ELX_SCALE=1` leaves screen buys at pump / alarm only.
- **Forced rare types:** `... 8 30000 8 4 oak,elder,gambler,press` puts them in the tree rooms: all four used (press 26, oak 8, elder 20, gambler 3). Natural runs see them in 2 to 5 matches of 20.
- **Fights unchanged:** 20-match summary 6.35 rounds, 179 s, 0 stuck, 0 timeouts; wins per bot easy 0.13, normal 0.18, hard 0.13.
- **Cost:** whole bot sweep 0.8 ms alone, 1.46 ms with 10 matches in parallel; every feature under 0.5 ms (`tests/bots5-sim`, `tests/bots`).
- **Tests:** `tests/botuse.test.js` (tally, screen buy through the range gate, re-arm and ammo, station tour, Oak nook, stats). `npm run test:affected`: only balance / bots-ai / combat timing flakes under load, all pass alone. `bots5` and `bots` pass alone.
