# Bots 2: bots that behave like players

Code: `server.js`, new block `AI-BEGIN/END` (inside `BOTS`, right after `FAIR-END`) plus edits to `newBrain`, `nearestEnemy`, `face`, `stepTo`, `follow`, `routeTo`, `voteDoor`, `lootNearby`, `pickDest`, `botThink` and the debug `botLab`. Tests: `tests/bots-ai.test.js` (new). Movement still goes only through `applyMove()` (new wrapper `mv()` adds the cosmetic jump arc as the 8th argument). The FAIR features are untouched: gang cap of 2 with the rest holding at 3.6 m, notice delay `BOT_NOTICE`, `BOT_MISS` 0.3 deliberate misses, mystery enemies (`p.npc`) stay in their room. Mystery enemies keep the old omniscient brain (`nearestEnemy`, `npcFight`); everything below is for bots (`p.bot`).

## What a bot does now
1. **Perception.** `perceive()` runs on every reaction tick (200-400 ms). A bot sees a goblin only if it is in the same room and noticed (FAIR delay) and: within 2.5 m in any direction, or inside a 150 degree cone in front of its yaw within 12 m, or it hit the bot in the last 3 s. Where it saw someone is kept per target (`brain.mem`); when the target drops out of view (and was seen in the last 4 s, same room) the bot walks to the last seen spot and looks around (`brain.search`). Idle bots (`idleLook`) turn 60-180 degrees over 0.5 s every 1-3 s, through `applyMove` at the same x,z; walking bots face their walking direction. After a route ends a bot now stands for 0.5-2.2 s (and scans) instead of picking the next wander point at once.
2. **Movement.** `brain.speed` is a current speed: sprint (7.5 x SPEED[goblin]) when chasing from far, fleeing or travelling between rooms (routes that cross a door); walk (4.6 x SPEED) for looting, wandering, searching, orbiting. Melee (`botFight`): orbit at 1.2-1.8 m, strafe direction flips every 0.6-1.5 s (85 % of the flips reverse), facing the target while moving sideways, a wall in the way reverses it; after about a third of its own swings it steps back 1-2 m, then sprints back in. Ranged bots (bows, staff/scepter bolts) keep 5-8 m and sidestep (flip every 0.5-1.1 s). One validated move per tick. Jumps (`jy` up to 90 cm, a 0.5 s arc): 12 % chance when the bot has just crossed a doorway, 50 % after a kill, at most one every 30-60 s per bot.
3. **Personality** (rolled in `newBrain`): `aggr` 0.3-1.0 (engagement range 4.5-8 m, nerve to start a fight with a stronger goblin), `flee` 15-40 % HP (replaces the flat 25 %; `caution` is the same roll as 0-1), `greed` (loot walking range 8-20 m, merchant and chest scores, merchant door weight), `patience` (vote moment in the round, doorway wait of `patience x 2` s, how late it heads for the vault in sudden death).
4. **Judgement.** Target score = distance + gang/human penalties (FAIR) + a weakness term (`sqrt(health x attack)` of both sides, stronger targets cost more for timid bots) - a wounded bonus. A goblin beyond 2.5 m that is more than `1.8 + 6 x aggr` times as dangerous and has not hit the bot is not picked on. If the bot's HP fraction drops 15 points and 1.4x faster than the target's over 1.8 s against someone stronger, it retreats (probability `0.3 + 0.7 x caution` per decision) through the NEAREST open door, sprinting, and avoids that room for 10-20 s (`pickDest` penalty); the HP flee goes through the door farthest from the target as before. A bot that enters a room and sees 2+ enemies holds near the doorway (looking around) for `patience x 2` s, taking only fights that come to it (within 2.5 m or hit it). After a kill: 0.5-1.2 s pause, 30 % emote broadcast as `{emote:{id, n}}` to the match and the spectators exactly like `socEmote` (sender rate limit `EMOTE_MS` kept), 50 % hop.
5. **Being hit.** A hit from outside the view (more than 2.5 m away and outside the cone, e.g. arrows and bolts) makes the bot "blind" to the attacker for 250-500 ms, then it turns to face him (and only then does the 3 s hit-sight rule recognise him). Two hits within 5 s while it was looting/claiming stop looting for 10 s and it flees (probability `caution`, if the attacker is not clearly weaker) or fights.
6. **Door votes.** Weights follow intent: wounded (< 50 % HP) bots x2.5 on quiet `chest` rooms (x0.3 for ones they already emptied, x0.25 for enemy mystery rooms, x0.5 vault); bots with gold >= 60 x(3 + 2 greed) on the merchant, x0.5 when poor; round 4+ still pushes the vault and the doors toward it. Vote moment = `(0.05..0.35 + patience x 0.15..0.5)` of the remaining round (was 0.1..0.8 flat). Impatient bots (45 % x (1 - patience)) re-roll their own vote 1.2-4.8 s before the round ends, to a different door; only if `selBy` is the bot itself and no human is in the room (the initial vote still requires no human in the room and no earlier vote). Sudden death: every bot gets a personal `sdAt` (0.1..1 patience scale x 1-9 s); the +7 vault bonus in `pickDest` only applies after it (measured: five bots reached the vault 3.1-6.6 s after sudden death started).
7. **Route safety.** Doorway waypoints remember their door; `follow()` drops the route (and re-plans) when that door closed meanwhile. Before this, a bot walking into a just-closed door produced refused `applyMove` calls (up to 16 in 70 s of 8-bot play with 6 s rounds); now 0 in three 70 s observation runs.

## Debug additions (GK_DEBUG only)
`botlab`: `{near:[x,z], yaw, scanAfter, wpn}` (yaw in radians, `scanAfter` ms before an idle bot first looks around, `wpn` a weapon key or `'none'` for unarmed); ring bots now face the middle by default; the reply carries `yaw` and `ai` (the personality). `GK_BOT_EMOTE_P` overrides the 30 % emote chance (tests use 1).

## Tests
`tests/bots-ai.test.js`: (a) a goblin 4 m directly behind an idle bot (facing away, `scanAfter` 1 s) is not attacked within 0.8 s and is attacked within 10 s (the bot scans); (b) bot snapshots exceed 5.5 m/s while travelling (server with 3 s rounds so doors open), jump height <= 90 cm and at most one jump per 30 s; (c) 6 s melee against a still human (bot unarmed so the test human survives): lateral path > 1.5 m (measured 4-20 m), within 0.8-2.6 m at least 60 % of the time (measured 82-100 %), at least one direction reversal; (d) with `GK_BOT_EMOTE_P=1`, the bot that killed the human broadcasts `{emote:{id:bot, n:0..3}}`; (e) arrow from 5 m behind: the bot turns to the shooter 390-710 ms after the hit; (f) personalities are in range and differ; (g) `GK_SIM=1` runs `node tests/sim-fairness.js 3 1` and requires mean <= 3 (3 minutes, so it is skipped in `npm test`, whose files time out at 60 s). The existing bot tick cost test (`tests/bots.test.js`) passes: mean 0.52-0.77 ms per sweep for 64 bots, max about 2 ms.

Fairness simulation (`node tests/sim-fairness.js 3 1`, tier 5 gear, 9 s rounds, sudden death from round 3): before **2, 1, 2 of 8** (docs/reports/balance.md; 2 in a fresh run), after **2, 2, 1** (mean 1.7) and **3, 2, 2** (mean 2.3). Bots now rarely start a fight with a human wearing tier 5 gear (about 7x as dangerous as a start-gear bot) unless provoked, so this number can only get easier for a strong human; it says nothing about how fair weak or mid-gear humans have it.

`npm test`: 20 files pass (31.6 s, run twice). One change to an existing test: `tests/balance.test.js` "deliberate misses" was flaky on the unmodified code too (4 of 8 runs failed with the original `server.js`: when the bot rolls a mace or club and the human a 40-50 HP class, the human dies after three swings, so "at least 6 swings" or "not every swing hit" fails by chance). It now pools swings over up to 6 fresh matches (target 12 swings, 6 s each) and asserts the same two things. This is not a behaviour change of the bots.
