Privacy and terms

Goblin King. Last updated 2026-10-16.

The short version

Goblin King has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. You do not give us a name, an email address or a password.

What your browser keeps

The game stores a few things in your own browser so it works the next time you visit. They never leave your computer except where noted.

  • Settings (graphics, volume, controls, tips, accessibility options, whether you finished the tutorial, your chat preference): localStorage.
  • Your goblins and stash, including a signed token that proves your victories to the server: localStorage. The token is sent to the game server when you play.
  • Your match statistics: localStorage.
  • A reconnect ticket so a page reload or a short connection drop puts you back into your match (about 30 seconds), and the private room code you chose: sessionStorage, gone when you close the tab.
  • Baked item icons, a cache only: IndexedDB.

To delete all of it, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your goblins are then gone for good: there is no copy on the server.

What the server sees

  • Your IP address, while you are connected, to limit connections per address and stop abuse. It is kept in memory only and not written to disk by the game.
  • What you do in a match (moves, picks, trades). It lives in memory for the length of the match.
  • The goblin name you type. Names are filtered and shown to the other players in your match.
  • Quick chat: you pick one of eight fixed phrases (there is no free text). The server passes the phrase to the other players in your match and does not store it.
  • Private room codes: a code you type or share is part of the connection and kept in memory while the room exists.
  • Reports: if a player reports another, the server writes one log line with both goblin names (not IP addresses), the match number, the reason and the time.

Bots (computer-controlled goblins) fill empty seats in a match; they are marked as bots in the match and in the queue. They are not people and no data is collected from them.

The hosting provider may keep its own access logs.

Third parties

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address and browser when the page opens.

Future telemetry

If we ever add gameplay statistics, they will be anonymous, described here first, and you will be able to switch them off in Settings.

Muting and reporting

Press H in a match to mute another player's quick chat and emotes for yourself, or to report them. Muting is only stored for the current page visit.

Terms of play

  • Goblin King is free to play and offered as is, with no warranty. Matches, goblins and stashes can be lost or reset at any time.
  • Be decent. Offensive goblin names are replaced automatically. Cheating, flooding the server or attacking it gets you disconnected.
  • You must be old enough to use online games in your country, or play with a parent's permission.
